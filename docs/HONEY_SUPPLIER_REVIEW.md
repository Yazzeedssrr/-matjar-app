## Required written response before approval

1. Two or three exact honey SKUs, sample labels and ingredients/origin, net weight, traceability/recall process and evidence for any organic/raw claim.
2. Cost for one unit and multi-unit order to Michigan plus representative near/far destinations; packing, shipping, surcharges and tax treatment.
3. Single-unit dropship eligibility, neutral packaging and permission to resell/use images.
4. Current stock retrieval, change frequency, reservation and out-of-stock handling; no quantity invented.
5. Supplier payment timing, accepted payment method, cancellation cutoff, and refund method (cash versus account credit).
6. Dispatch, carrier tracking, damage/loss evidence deadline, remedy and responsibility for customer replacement costs.
